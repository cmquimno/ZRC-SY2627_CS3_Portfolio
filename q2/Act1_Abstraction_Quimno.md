# Activity 1: The Digital Scale (Electronic Balance) Analysis

**Core Concept:** **Abstraction** separates the *Interface* (what we use) from the *Implementation* (what is hidden).

### 1. The Interface (The Abstraction)
These are the only elements a scientist needs to interact with to get a result:

* **The Power Button:** Turns the system on/off.
* **The Tare/Zero Button:** Tells the scale to ignore the weight of the beaker.
* **The Display Screen:** Shows the final number in grams.

### 2. The Implementation (The Hidden Complexity)
These are the complex operations happening inside the box that are hidden from our view:

* **The Load Cell:** A metal sensor that bends slightly under weight.
* **Signal Processing:** Converting a tiny electrical pulse into a digital number.
* **Calibration Math:** The internal formulas that ensure 1g on the scale is actually 1g in the real world.
